## 1) Voice “Start My Day” Briefing
- **Title:** Voice “Start My Day” Briefing
- **Customer Problem:** Busy or low-vision users must visually parse the screen each morning to know what matters now.
- **Customer Value:** One tap gives a short spoken brief (next key reminders, quiet status, quick encouragement), making daily startup feel easy and warm.
- **Likely Surface:** Home face `/` primary action button, using existing reminder/chime/speech pipeline.
- **Why Net-New:** Prior rounds covered reminders, boards, and personalization, but not a dedicated spoken daily briefing ritual.

## 2) Hands-Free Voice Actions for Active Reminders
- **Title:** Hands-Free Voice Actions for Active Reminders
- **Customer Problem:** When cooking, caregiving, or moving around the house, tapping `Done`/`Snooze` is inconvenient or inaccessible.
- **Customer Value:** Users can say simple commands like “done,” “snooze 10,” or “repeat,” improving completion and accessibility without extra navigation.
- **Likely Surface:** Active reminder state on `/`, mapped to existing reminder action model in schedule flows.
- **Why Net-New:** Earlier rounds proposed actionable reminder buttons and collaboration, but not voice command control.

## 3) Encouragement Micro-Moments After Completions
- **Title:** Encouragement Micro-Moments After Completions
- **Customer Problem:** Routine completion feels mechanical, so motivation drops over time even when tasks are getting done.
- **Customer Value:** Brief positive audio/text feedback after completions builds emotional reward and stickiness with minimal UI weight.
- **Likely Surface:** Home completion state on `/`, optional tone/voice style toggles in `/brain`.
- **Why Net-New:** Previous ideas touched goals/streaks and nudge style, but not lightweight celebratory feedback moments tied to everyday wins.

## 4) Spoken Catch-Up Digest (“What Did I Miss?”)
- **Title:** Spoken Catch-Up Digest (“What Did I Miss?”)
- **Customer Problem:** After quiet mode, dictation, or returning to the tablet, users may miss context and need to read small details quickly.
- **Customer Value:** A one-tap spoken digest summarizes missed and upcoming items, reducing cognitive load for low-vision and multitasking households.
- **Likely Surface:** Home face `/` catch-up card/button, backed by existing missed-reminder/catch-up event logic.
- **Why Net-New:** Earlier rounds proposed visual missed digests, but not an audio-first digest flow designed for accessibility and warmth.
