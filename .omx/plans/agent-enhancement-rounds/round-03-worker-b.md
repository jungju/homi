## 1) Time-Shifted Routine Suggestions
- **Title:** Time-Shifted Routine Suggestions
- **Customer Problem:** Households often set reminder times once, then keep snoozing because real life timing drifts (school pickup, dinner, bedtime variability).
- **Customer Value:** Homi suggests better times per routine based on actual completion/snooze behavior, so reminders land when people are most likely to act.
- **Likely Surface:** Suggestion chip on `/` after repeated snoozes, schedule item “suggested time update” in `/engines/schedule`, opt-in controls in `/brain`.
- **Why Net-New:** Earlier rounds covered recurrence and reminder actions, but not behavior-driven timing optimization that adapts schedules automatically.

## 2) Adaptive Dictation Difficulty Ladder
- **Title:** Adaptive Dictation Difficulty Ladder
- **Customer Problem:** Fixed dictation sets are either too easy or too hard over time, which causes boredom or frustration and breaks daily practice.
- **Customer Value:** Practice stays in a “just-right” zone by auto-adjusting pace, prompt complexity, and review frequency based on recent accuracy and hesitation.
- **Likely Surface:** Dictation session engine in `/engines/dictation`, “level up/down” explanation card on `/`, adaptation preferences in `/brain`.
- **Why Net-New:** Prior rounds proposed goals/streaks and quick queues, but not per-user skill calibration and dynamic difficulty progression.

## 3) Personalized Next-Best Action Card
- **Title:** Personalized Next-Best Action Card
- **Customer Problem:** On busy days, families see many possible tasks and don’t know which single action gives the most benefit right now.
- **Customer Value:** Homi surfaces one high-impact “do this now” suggestion using overdue status, time-of-day, and routine importance to reduce decision friction.
- **Likely Surface:** Primary card on home face `/`, rationale tooltip (“why this now”), priority tuning in `/brain`.
- **Why Net-New:** Earlier ideas included boards and persistent reminders, but not ranked recommendation logic that picks the most helpful immediate action.

## 4) Nudge Style Personalization
- **Title:** Nudge Style Personalization
- **Customer Problem:** A single reminder tone/channel does not work for everyone; some ignore chimes, others ignore text, and repetitive prompts become noise.
- **Customer Value:** Homi adapts how it nudges (speech/chime/text emphasis, phrasing style, urgency) based on what each household actually responds to.
- **Likely Surface:** Home reminder rendering on `/`, schedule reminder options in `/engines/schedule`, “nudge profile” settings and learning opt-in in `/brain`.
- **Why Net-New:** Previous rounds addressed delivery checks and channel reliability, but not response-based personalization of nudge style for higher daily follow-through.
