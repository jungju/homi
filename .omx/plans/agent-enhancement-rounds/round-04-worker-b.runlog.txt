## 1) Seasonal Routine Packs and Auto-Rotation
- **Title:** Seasonal Routine Packs and Auto-Rotation
- **Customer Problem:** Households burn out on seeing the same routine framing all year, even when the core tasks stay relevant.
- **Customer Value:** Keeps Homi feeling current by rotating routine copy, icons, and optional prompts by season/back-to-school/holiday periods without forcing full reconfiguration.
- **Likely Surface:** `/brain` seasonal pack selector and rotation rules, home face `/` seasonal routine presentation, optional per-item seasonal variants in `/engines/schedule`.
- **Why Net-New:** Earlier rounds covered starter packs and adaptive timing, but not calendar-based freshness layers that refresh experience without changing reminder logic.

## 2) Routine Energy Budget with Built-In Light Days
- **Title:** Routine Energy Budget with Built-In Light Days
- **Customer Problem:** Daily max-effort expectations cause habit collapse; families need sustainable pacing, not constant intensity.
- **Customer Value:** Prevents fatigue by letting households define weekly “light” days and maximum active routine load so Homi automatically scales down non-critical prompts.
- **Likely Surface:** `/brain` weekly sustainability settings, schedule item intensity tags in `/engines/schedule`, home `/` “light day” indicator and reduced task stack.
- **Why Net-New:** Prior rounds proposed quiet controls and personalization, but not explicit anti-burnout pacing that manages total routine load across the week.

## 3) Stale Routine Detector with Refresh Suggestions
- **Title:** Stale Routine Detector with Refresh Suggestions
- **Customer Problem:** Long-running routines quietly decay when repeatedly snoozed/skipped, but households don’t notice until they stop using Homi.
- **Customer Value:** Protects long-term engagement by identifying stale routines and offering practical refresh actions (shorten, split, change time window, pause temporarily, rewrite wording).
- **Likely Surface:** `/brain` “Needs Refresh” panel, schedule editor `/engines/schedule` one-tap refresh suggestions, home `/` gentle refresh prompt.
- **Why Net-New:** Existing ideas adapt timing and tone, but none focus on lifecycle maintenance of aging routines with explicit refresh workflows.

## 4) Content Remix Studio for Dictation and Reminder Language
- **Title:** Content Remix Studio for Dictation and Reminder Language
- **Customer Problem:** Repeated wording becomes mentally invisible; users tune out familiar phrases and dictation prompts over time.
- **Customer Value:** Keeps attention high by generating rotating phrasing variants (gentle, direct, playful, concise) and fresh dictation prompt sets from existing household content.
- **Likely Surface:** `/brain` remix controls and style presets, dictation content tools in `/engines/dictation`, reminder text variant toggle in `/engines/schedule`, live usage on `/`.
- **Why Net-New:** Prior rounds included nudge style personalization and adaptive dictation difficulty, but not systematic language/content remixing to combat habituation fatigue.
